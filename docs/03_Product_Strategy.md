# 03_Product_Strategy.md

## Executive Summary

Cerius is an AI-powered meal-planning app that generates personalized weekly meal plans and automated grocery lists for busy professionals. We target the $6B health/fitness market and the explosive growth in AI apps (1.7B downloads, $1.87B revenue in H1 2025).

**Business Model:** Freemium subscription ($5-8/month premium)  
**Target:** 10K+ downloads, 500+ subscribers, 4.8+ rating, 5%+ conversion  
**Timeline:** 30-day MVP launch  
**North Star Metric:** Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)

---

## Mission & Vision

**Mission:** Help busy adults eat healthier with zero meal-planning stress using AI.

**Vision:** Become the #1 trusted AI meal-planning app on Google Play.

**Core Values:**
- **Simplicity** - One tap to get a week of meals
- **Speed** - Instant personalized meal plans
- **Trust** - Accurate, safe nutritional guidance
- **Privacy** - User data never sold or misused
- **Reliability** - Plans always work, even offline

---

## Problem Statement

**User Pain Points:**
1. **Decision Fatigue:** "What should I cook this week?" is exhausting
2. **Time Constraint:** Busy professionals don't have time to plan meals daily
3. **Waste:** Buying wrong ingredients or duplicates wastes money
4. **Lack of Variety:** Users get stuck in meal ruts
5. **Dietary Complexity:** Managing restrictions (allergies, vegetarian, keto, etc.) is hard

**Market Opportunity:**
- Health/fitness apps grew ~24% in 2025 (annual revenue ~$6B)
- AI apps hit 1.7B downloads and $1.87B revenue in H1 2025 alone
- Subscription apps prove users will pay: Google One = $2.6B ARR, ChatGPT is #1 grossing app
- But few apps focus purely on **AI-automated meal planning**

## Competitor Analysis & Positioning

| App | Strength | Weakness | Our Advantage |
|-----|----------|----------|---------------|
| **Mealime** (1M+ DL, $2.99/mo) | Polished UI, great personalization | Limited free tier, some sloppy recipes | Better AI integration |
| **Samsung Food** (1M+ DL, Free/$5) | Massive recipe DB, trusted brand | Charges for AI features, some UX issues | Simpler, pure AI focus |

| **Lifesum** (10M DL, $5-10/mo) | Massive user base, comprehensive | Too broad focus, expensive, complex | Simpler, focused on meal planning only |

### Positioning: "Simplicity First, AI-Powered"

**Unique Value Proposition:**
> "Get a week of personalized meals in 30 seconds. No planning. No stress. Just order groceries and cook."

**Why Choose Cerius Over Competitors:**
1. **Purest AI Experience** - We optimize for AI automation, not comprehensive tracking
2. **Simplicity** - One flow: Profile → Plan → Groceries. That's it.
3. **Fastest Generation** - AI meal plans in <5 seconds
4. **Best Personalization** - Learns from user preferences, not just static rules
5. **Reliability** - Built for professionals who demand no friction

## Business Model

### Pricing Strategy

**Free Tier:**
- 1 meal plan generation per week
- View recipes from generated plans
- Basic grocery list
- No advanced filters

**Premium Tier ($5.99/month or $49.99/year):**
- Unlimited meal plan generations
- Advanced dietary filters (macro targets, specialty diets)
- Batch grocery lists (plan 2-4 weeks ahead)
- Recipe preferences (save favorites, exclude disliked meals)
- Premium recipe integration
- Priority support
- No ads

**Rationale:**
- Competitive with Mealime ($2.99), Lifesum ($5-10)
- Freemium model proves demand before commitment
- $5.99 = $72 ARR per subscriber (below fitness app average of ~$100)
- Monthly option for impulse conversion, annual option for retention

### Revenue Model

**Primary:** Subscription revenue (MRR → ARR)
**Secondary (Future):** Affiliate links to grocery delivery, premium recipe sources
**Tertiary (Future):** B2B licensing for corporate wellness programs

## MVP Scope

### In-Scope (Must Have)

**Core User Flows:**
1. Install → Email/Google Sign-up → Complete Profile
2. Profile → Generate Meal Plan → Review Plan
3. View Plan → View Recipes → View Grocery List
4. Grocery List → Checkout/Share
5. Manage Profile → Update Preferences

**Core Features:**
- [ ] Email/Google OAuth authentication
- [ ] User profile setup (goals, restrictions, dislikes, caloric targets)
- [ ] AI meal plan generation (powered by OpenAI GPT-4)
- [ ] Weekly meal plan display (7-day calendar)
- [ ] Recipe view (name, ingredients, instructions, macros)
- [ ] Auto-generated grocery list (aggregated, by category)
- [ ] Grocery list checkout (mark items, share)
- [ ] Subscription purchase and management (Google Play Billing)
- [ ] Profile editing (update preferences, re-generate plan)
- [ ] Push notifications (weekly reminder to plan)
- [ ] Dark mode support
- [ ] Offline recipe viewing (cached plans)

**Quality Requirements:**
- [ ] Production-ready error handling
- [ ] Validation on all inputs
- [ ] Secure API communication (HTTPS, JWT)
- [ ] GDPR-compliant privacy policy
- [ ] Health disclaimer in onboarding
- [ ] Crash monitoring (Firebase Crashlytics)
- [ ] Analytics tracking (Firebase Analytics)
- [ ] Google Play Store submission compliance

## Intentionally Excluded from MVP

### Post-MVP Features (Phase 2)

**Personalization:**
- [ ] Favorite recipes library
- [ ] "Never show again" recipe feedback
- [ ] Meal ratings/feedback loop
- [ ] Improved AI model training on user preferences

**Social/Gamification:**
- [ ] Share meal plans with friends
- [ ] Social proof (see what others cooked)
- [ ] Badges and streaks
- [ ] Leaderboards or challenges

**Integrations:**
- [ ] Affiliate grocery delivery (Amazon Fresh, Instacart)
- [ ] Sync with MyFitnessPal or fitness trackers
- [ ] Recipe source integrations (AllRecipes, Food Network)
- [ ] Smart list sync (Google Keep, Todoist)

**Advanced Features:**
- [ ] Detailed nutrition analysis (macro/micronutrient breakdowns)
- [ ] Recipe scaling based on servings
- [ ] Budget-optimized meal planning
- [ ] Seasonal recipe suggestions
- [ ] Multi-person household meal planning
- [ ] Pantry management (what you have at home)
- [ ] Cooking technique tutorials

**Platforms:**
- [ ] iOS app (after Android MVP)
- [ ] Web dashboard (after mobile stable)
- [ ] Apple Watch app
- [ ] Alexa/Google Home integrations

**Why Excluded:**
These features add complexity, increase API costs, and distract from core MVP goal: **nail the core loop of "generate → view → subscribe"** in 30 days.

## Risk Assessment & Mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-------------|-----------|
| OpenAI API rate limits/costs exceed projections | High | Medium | Pre-plan daily/monthly budgets; implement caching and plan reuse |
| Poor AI meal quality (repetitive, irrelevant plans) | High | Medium | Extensive prompt engineering; test with beta users; fallback to recipe database |
| Low conversion to paid (>5%) | High | Medium | Focus on free tier UX that hooks users; test IAP pricing; analyze cohort behavior |
| High churn (>10%/month) | High | Medium | Prioritize retention features (streaks, notifications); weekly engagement loops |
| Competitor adds similar AI features | High | Low | Speed to market; build data moat (user preferences); better UX |
| Nutritional accuracy concerns / liability | Medium | Medium | Legal disclaimer in onboarding; never claim medical advice; flag common allergens |
| Google Play rejection / policy violations | Medium | Low | Pre-review all policies; test submission; prepare appeal strategy |
| Development delays (30-day timeline aggressive) | Medium | Medium | Strict scope management; daily standups; cut features ruthlessly if behind |
| User data breach / privacy violation | High | Low | Encrypt all data in transit/at rest; minimal data collection; regular audits |
| Firebase/MongoDB downtime impacts production | Medium | Low | Use multiple regions; implement fallback caching; monitor SLAs |

## Key Assumptions

1. **OpenAI API remains affordable** (~$0.03 per meal plan at scale)
2. **5-8% conversion to paid is achievable** (based on competitive benchmarks)
3. **30-day build is feasible** with focused scope and experienced team
4. **Android/Capacitor can deliver native-quality UX** (no major performance bottlenecks)
5. **Users will generate plans weekly** (core retention behavior)
6. **Meal personalization reduces churn** vs. generic meal planners
7. **App Store Optimization works** to drive organic downloads
8. **Health/fitness audience trusts AI** for meal suggestions

---

## Critical Success Factors

1. **Nail onboarding** - Get users to first meal plan in <2 minutes
2. **Make premium obvious value** - Free tier should create desire to upgrade
3. **Ensure meal quality** - Bad plans = uninstalls and negative reviews
4. **Weekly engagement loop** - Users return every Sunday to plan
5. **Blazing fast experience** - Slow app = churn
6. **Beautiful design** - Competitors are polished; we must match
7. **Flawless app stability** - Crashes are deal-breakers for productivity apps
